Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the 30-day free trial? ▼
The free trial includes full access to all features of your selected plan with no credit card required. You can create studies, use AI matching, generate questionnaires, and explore all platform capabilities. At the end of 30 days, you can choose to subscribe or your account will simply expire with no charges.
Can I change plans later? ▼
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to new features. When downgrading, changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and you'll retain access to your current plan features until then.
What happens if I exceed my study limit? ▼
If you approach your study limit, we'll notify you in advance. You can either upgrade to a higher tier or archive completed studies to make room for new ones. We'll never interrupt your active studies or charge surprise fees.
Is my data secure and compliant? ▼
Absolutely. FeasiQuest is HIPAA compliant, SOC 2 certified, FDA aligned, and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We undergo regular security audits and maintain strict access controls. Your data is yours and will never be shared without your explicit permission.
Do you offer discounts for multiple years? ▼
Yes! Annual billing already includes a 20% discount. For multi-year commitments (2-3 years), we offer additional discounts of 5-10%. Contact our sales team for custom pricing for long-term partnerships.
What kind of support do you provide? ▼
All plans include email support with response times based on your tier (48-72hr for Starter, 24-48hr for Professional, 12hr for Enterprise). Enterprise customers also get phone support, a dedicated account manager, and quarterly business reviews. We also provide comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, and a knowledge base.
