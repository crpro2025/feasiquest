## Phase 9: Objection Handling

### Common Objections & Responses

**Objection 1: "We already have a feasibility process"**
- **Response:** "That's great! Most companies do. The question is: How long does it take and how much does it cost? Our customers were spending $500K-$2M per study and taking 3-6 months. With FeasiQuest, they're spending $2,000 per study and taking 1 week. Would you be open to seeing how we compare?"

**Objection 2: "We're not ready to change vendors"**
- **Response:** "I completely understand. This is a 30-day free trial with no commitment. You can test it alongside your current process and see the difference. If it doesn't save you time and money, you can walk away. Fair?"

**Objection 3: "The pricing seems high"**
- **Response:** "I appreciate that concern. Let me put it in perspective: If you're running 10 studies per year at $500K each for site selection, that's $5M. FeasiQuest costs $19,990/year. That's a $4.98M savings - a 24,900% ROI. Does that change the equation?"

**Objection 4: "We need to see more proof"**
- **Response:** "Absolutely. That's exactly why we have the beta program. You'll get 30 days to test it with your own studies, see real results, and then decide. Plus, we have case studies from similar companies. Would you like to see those?"

**Objection 5: "We need buy-in from other stakeholders"**
- **Response:** "That makes total sense. Would it be helpful if I presented to your team? I can do a 15-minute demo that shows the ROI and addresses any concerns. What's the best way to get everyone together?"

**Objection 6: "We're concerned about data security"**
- **Response:** "Security is our top priority. We're HIPAA compliant, SOC 2 certified, FDA aligned, and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We undergo regular security audits. Would you like to see our security documentation?"
