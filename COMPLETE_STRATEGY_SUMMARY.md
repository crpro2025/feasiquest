## 5. DESIGN REFRESH RECOMMENDATIONS

### **Current Design Issues:**
- ❌ Red/purple gradient too bold and "startup-y"
- ❌ Neon cyan/purple feels gaming/tech, not healthcare
- ❌ Black backgrounds too dark for professional use
- ❌ Lacks trust signals and credibility

### **Recommended Professional Design:**

**Color Palette:**
- **Primary:** Deep Navy (#1B365D) - Trust, stability
- **Secondary:** Medical Blue (#0077C8) - Healthcare standard
- **Accent:** Sage Green (#4A7C59) - Growth, health
- **Neutral:** Warm Gray (#6B7280) - Professional
- **Background:** Clean White (#FFFFFF) - Clarity

**Typography:**
- **Font:** Inter (modern, professional, excellent readability)
- **Headings:** 600-700 weight
- **Body:** 400-500 weight
- **Sizes:** 16px base, 36-48px headings

**Design Principles:**
- Clean white backgrounds with subtle shadows
- Professional photography (real people, real sites)
- Certifications and compliance badges prominent
- Minimal animations (subtle, purposeful)
- High contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA)

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Conveys trust and credibility
- ✅ Aligns with healthcare industry standards
- ✅ Appeals to enterprise buyers
- ✅ Professional, not "startup-y"
- ✅ Builds confidence with decision makers
