## 7. PRICING & PACKAGING STRATEGY

### **Pricing Philosophy:**

**1. Value-Based Pricing:**
- Price based on value delivered, not cost
- Sponsors save $1M+ per study → charge $25K/year
- Sites save 40 hours/month → charge $3K/year

**2. Transparent Pricing:**
- All prices published on website
- No "contact us for pricing"
- Builds trust with buyers

**3. Land-and-Expand:**
- Start with small commitment (1-2 studies)
- Expand as they see value
- Upsell to higher tiers

**4. Annual Discounts:**
- 20% discount for annual payment
- Improves cash flow
- Reduces churn

### **Packaging Strategy:**

**For Sponsors:**
- **Starter:** Perfect for 1-2 studies (test the platform)
- **Professional:** Ideal for 5-15 studies (most mid-size pharma)
- **Enterprise:** For 20+ studies (large pharma, CROs)

**For Sites:**
- **Free:** Get started, see value (lead generation)
- **Professional:** For active sites (5-10 studies/year)
- **Premium:** For high-volume sites (15+ studies/year)

### **Discounting Strategy:**

**Beta Customers:**
- 50% off first year (lock in early adopters)
- Full price Year 2 (after they see value)

**Annual Prepay:**
- 20% discount (standard)
- Improves cash flow and retention

**Multi-Year Contracts:**
- Year 1: Full price
- Year 2: 10% discount
- Year 3: 15% discount

**Volume Discounts (CROs):**
- 5-10 studies: Standard pricing
- 11-25 studies: 10% discount
- 26-50 studies: 15% discount
- 51+ studies: 20% discount + custom terms
